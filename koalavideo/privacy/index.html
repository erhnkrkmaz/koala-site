Koala Privacy Policy

How Koala collects, uses, shares, and deletes information.

Effective August 16, 2026

Who controls your information

Koala Tech AI Inc. operates Koala and is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. Questions and privacy requests may be sent to info@heykoala.app. The current policy is also available through heykoala.app.

Account and profile information

We collect the Sign in with Apple identifier used to authenticate you and any name or email Apple shares according to your Apple settings. We also collect your required profile photo, name, gender selection, self-reported age, bio, U.S. city, Instagram handle, interests, agreement version and acceptance time, and profile review status.

Calls, matching, and usage

When you use video matching, we process queue and match events, private channel identifiers, participant identifiers, call join and leave times, duration, Next/skip events, connection results, and coarse interaction outcomes. Interests, bio text, city, gender, age range, prior matches, blocks, skips, and quality signals may be used to rank compatible matches and improve the system over time.

Purchases and referrals

For subscriptions, we process Apple-signed transaction and original-transaction identifiers, product, environment, purchase and expiration times, revocation status, and an opaque app-account token that links the purchase to your Koala account. Koala does not receive your full payment-card details. For referral eligibility and fraud prevention, we store invite code, qualification and reward times, related account UUIDs, and a one-way hash of the Sign in with Apple provider identifier. The raw Apple provider identifier is not stored in the referral ledger. This limited pseudonymous anti-replay record may remain after account deletion to prevent repeated delete-and-recreate rewards, protect the service, and resolve disputes; it is not used to rebuild your deleted profile or for advertising.

Video, audio, and on-device analysis

After iOS permission is granted, live audio and front-camera video are transmitted through Agora to the matched participant. During an active call, sampled local and remote video frames may be analyzed on your iPhone by Koala's built-in NudeNet model for possible nudity. Frames that do not trigger an incident are not uploaded to or stored by Koala. Koala does not start a call if its required built-in analysis cannot load. We process limited call-presence metadata to establish private calls, prevent unauthorized access, create verified post-call connections, and support reporting and blocking.

Safety evidence and human review

If repeated automated analysis indicates possible sensitive content, or if a member selects the nudity report reason, Koala may capture and upload one compressed video frame from the incident. The call ends immediately. The image, detector metadata, call identifiers, report context, account status, and moderator decision are stored in access-restricted Supabase systems for human review. Automated detection is a signal, not a final decision: a false positive may be cleared and access restored; a confirmed violation may result in suspension. Reports automatically create a block, but a manual report by itself does not automatically suspend the reported account.

Messages and other safety information

We store direct-message content and metadata so participants can communicate and receive notifications. We also store blocks, reports, report details, moderation decisions, and related call or conversation context. Authorized personnel may review safety information to investigate abuse and enforce Koala's rules.

Device and technical information

We may process push-notification tokens, notification preference, app version, device and operating-system information, network and service diagnostics, timestamps, IP-derived security information, and crash or error data needed to authenticate requests, deliver messages, protect accounts, debug failures, and control costs and reliability.

How we use information

We use information to create and review profiles; authenticate members; provide calls and direct messages; personalize and evaluate matching; deliver notifications; prevent duplicate, blocked, or unsafe connections; investigate reports; enforce rules; maintain security and reliability; measure product performance; comply with law; and communicate important service or policy changes.

How information is shared

Approved members see the profile fields you submit. A connected member sees messages you send. Live call participants receive your audio and video during the call. We disclose information to service providers that operate Koala, including Apple for sign-in and push notifications, Supabase for authentication, database, storage, realtime, and server functions, and Agora for realtime calls. We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and service integrity.

No sale or targeted advertising

Koala does not sell personal information and does not use profile, call, or message information for cross-context behavioral advertising in this version. If that practice changes, we will update this policy and provide any legally required choices before the change takes effect.

Retention

Account, profile, connection, and message information is generally retained while your account or the relevant feature remains active. Deleting a chat clears your copy from that point forward but may not erase the other participant's copy or safety evidence. Sensitive-content evidence is scheduled for deletion seven days after a cleared incident and ninety days after a confirmed incident; pending evidence is initially scheduled for thirty days but may be preserved longer when reasonably necessary to finish a review, respond to an appeal, protect a person, comply with law, or resolve a dispute. Account deletion removes the account's active evidence objects in the normal deletion flow, subject to any legally required preservation. Other limited fraud-prevention, legal, dispute, and provider-backup records may remain only as long as reasonably necessary and are then deleted or de-identified.

Your choices and rights

You can change camera, microphone, photo, and notification access in iOS Settings; turn Koala notifications off in the app; delete your own messages or chat copy; block and unblock members; sign out; and permanently delete your account in Settings. Depending on where you live, you may also request access, correction, deletion, portability, or information about processing and may have the right to appeal a decision or complain to a regulator.

California privacy rights

Where California law applies, you may request to know, access, correct, or delete covered personal information and may exercise applicable opt-out or limitation rights without discriminatory treatment. Koala does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising in this version. Submit a verifiable request to info@heykoala.app.

Security and international processing

We use access controls, private storage, short-lived media links, row-level authorization, server-issued call credentials, and encrypted network transport designed to protect information. No system is completely secure. Service providers may process information in the United States or other locations subject to appropriate contractual and legal safeguards.

Changes and contact

We will update the effective date and present material changes when appropriate. Contact Koala Tech AI Inc. at info@heykoala.app or visit heykoala.app. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or sensitive financial information by email.

Koala is operated by Koala Tech AI Inc. Koala is an independent app.
